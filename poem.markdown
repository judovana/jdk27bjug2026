  Still Brewing II: A Java Ballad

  In Brno town where coders meet,
  A tale of Java, strong and fleet—
  Jiri Vanek took the stage,
  To turn the JDK's next page.

  JDK Twenty-Seven draws near,
  A Short-Term Support child, second this year,
  After Twenty-Five, the LTS throne,
  It carries features of its own.

  First, the headers, compact and tight,
  From ninety-eight bits to sixty-four light—
  Born when Red Hat asked one day,
  "Why does OpenJ9 outplay?"
  Now fifteen percent less heap you'll see,
  And five percent CPU running free.

  Post-Quantum keys now guard the gate,
  TLS one-point-three, secure of late—
  Lest quantum machines, in futures unclear,
  Break all the ciphers we hold dear.

  G1 now reigns as GC king,
  And JFR redacts each sensitive thing,
  While Structured Concurrency, patient and keen,
  Awaits in preview — its seventh scene.

  The Vector API, bless its soul,
  Twelve incubations, still not whole—
  SIMD dreams on Intel's shore,
  While ARM waits knocking at the door.
  The JIT compiler whispers low,
  "I optimize where vectors go."

  Lazy Constants find their groove,
  Stable Values on the move,
  And Primitives in Patterns play
  Their fifth preview without delay.

  Fear not! Old Swing still draws the frame,
  IntelliJ bears its ancient name—
  With Wayland, Metal, standing tall,
  A DatePicker comes to serve us all.

  Beyond the horizon, Twenty-Eight gleams,
  Valhalla rises from its dreams—
  Value Objects, lean and pure,
  Where abstraction meets performance cure.

  So raise your mugs, let Java pour,
  It's still brewing — there is more.
  From Brno's JUG to servers wide,
  The JDK marches on with pride.
